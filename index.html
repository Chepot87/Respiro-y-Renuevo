Private Wellness Experiences
in Puerto Rico

A different kind of Puerto Rico experience.

You didn't come to Puerto Rico just for the beach.
You came for something that stays with you.

We design private wellness experiences — half day, full day, or overnight — in Puerto Rico's most breathtaking natural settings. Guided by certified, locally based professionals. Coordinated entirely for you.

What to expect:

  • Certified wellness facilitators (bilingual available)
  • Authentic local venues — mountains, beaches, haciendas
  • Custom itinerary built around your group's intention
  • Full coordination — you just show up
  • Small groups only — intimate and intentional

Popular experiences for visitors:

  • Morning yoga + nature hike + healthy local meal
  • Emotional wellness half-day with certified facilitator
  • Overnight retreat in Puerto Rico's mountain region
  • Bachelorette or birthday wellness experience
  • Team retreat or executive off-site

All experiences are conducted by vetted, certified professionals. We work exclusively with locally owned venues and vendors.

Diseñamos experiencias privadas de bienestar para grupos que quieren algo más que un paseo — quieren una experiencia que se queda contigo.

¿Para qué tipo de grupos?

  • Cumpleaños o aniversarios
  • Grupos de amigas / despedidas de soltera con propósito
  • Equipos de trabajo o empresas
  • Grupos comunitarios o instituciones educativas
  • Familias

¿Qué incluye?

  • Facilitación por profesionales certificados
  • Locaciones locales seleccionadas (playas, haciendas, bosques)
  • Diseño de experiencia personalizada según tu intención
  • Coordinación completa — tú solo disfruta.
